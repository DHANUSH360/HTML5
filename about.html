VARALAKSHMI TIFFINS

     
                                                Varalakshmi Tiffins in Old Guntur is a well-known vegetarian hotel located near the Old Bus Stand on Main Road, Lalapet. 
                                                It is famous for its authentic South Indian dishes such as idlis, dosas, upma, and ghee-based varieties, which many customers 
                                                describe as homely and comforting. The prices are very budget-friendly, usually around ₹100 for two people, making it a popular
                                                choice for students and families alike.

                                                The branch offers dine-in, takeaway, and home delivery services to suit different needs. It operates from 7:00 AM to 11:30 AM for 
                                                breakfast and 4:30 PM to 12:00 midnight for dinner. Customers often appreciate the good taste, generous portions, and overall value
                                                for money. However, some reviews do point out inconsistencies in quality and quantity on certain occasions. Despite this, the Old
                                                Guntur branch maintains a strong reputation and continues to attract a regular crowd. It is considered one of the go-to spots for
                                                enjoying tasty and affordable tiffins in the city.
